# Aia Recovery and Site — recorded generation briefs v0.1

These are the final prompt sets used for the first robotics recovery scene and
the website social preview. Both outputs remain concept material.

## Scene 10 — robotics-assisted recovery

Create a cinematic premium 3D illustration in the established Oki Universe
visual language. At night on a dark blue digital sea, an OpenKubes platform
ship has capsized. Several clearly recognizable platform containers float in
the water. A coordinated team of friendly Aia robots — underwater drone,
flying drone, autonomous crane and deck robot — recovers the containers,
scans them and marks only verified cargo for transfer to a new intact ship.
Aia supervises from the recovery command point. Cops/Crossplane coordinates
the platform modules on the new deck; Captain/CAPI prepares the fresh cluster
ship. Make the movement from failed ship through verification to fresh ship
visually readable from left to right. Keep the mood hopeful and competent,
with deep navy, electric cyan, green and warm amber light. Robotics assists
recovery but does not bypass policy, identity or human authority. No disaster
victims, no military weapons, no illegible UI text, no extra logos.

Reference set: canonical Aia character, Captain concept sheet, Cops concept
sheet, OpenKubes platform ship and the supplied robotics reference image.

## Website social preview

Create one wide premium social-card illustration for the OpenKubes Aia
Illustrated Guide. Show Aia as the central protagonist with Captain/CAPI and
Cops/Crossplane beside her, against a refined deep-navy harbor and platform
ship backdrop. Preserve the established luminous 3D character style and
OpenKubes blue, green and amber palette. Use a clean editorial composition
with generous negative space and crisp, readable typography. Include exactly:
“OPENKUBES ILLUSTRATED GUIDE”, “AIA”, “Vom Wunsch zur verifizierbaren
Plattform.” and “AI · CROSSPLANE · CLUSTER API · EVIDENCE”. No additional
copy, no fake interface, no unrelated logos.

Reference set: Scene 05 concept, Captain concept sheet and Cops concept sheet.
