# Crossplane Conductor — recorded generation brief v0.1

## Character sheet

Use case: style-transfer

Transform the existing friendly orange beaver Crossplane character into
“Conductor — the Crossplane conductor”, preserving the recognizable beaver
identity, face, orange fur, proportions, goggles, hand-painted storybook
rendering, cream paper texture and 4-column by 2-row character-sheet structure.

Replace the workman vest with a refined deep teal conductor tailcoat with
subtle copper piping and small cloud/platform motifs. Add a slim wooden baton
and a rolled or open architectural Composition score made from abstract
resource symbols, without readable text. Retain practical boots and a small
tool belt so the character remains a controller/operator rather than a stage
magician.

The top row contains front, three-quarter, side and back views. The bottom row
shows Conductor reading the Composition score, conducting several small
platform-resource ensembles, reconciling blocks into harmony and collaborating
with Captain.

The baton symbolizes reconciliation, not command authority. Avoid police
imagery, Authority keys, crowns, wands, magic effects, weapons, logos and
readable labels. Preserve generous spacing and the Oki Universe watercolor and
gouache visual language.
