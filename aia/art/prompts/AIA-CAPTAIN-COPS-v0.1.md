## Captain / CAPI

Create a canonical-style Oki Universe character sheet for Captain, the
personified face of Kubernetes Cluster API. Captain is a small, friendly
navy-blue sea-captain and shipwright creature with teal fin-like hair,
expressive eyes, a weathered navy work jacket, sturdy boots, a white captain
cap and warm brass details. His tools communicate declarative cluster
lifecycle: wheel, compass, pictographic logbook and cluster-ship model. Show
front, three-quarter, side and back views plus steering, inspection, repair and
readiness poses. Match the canonical Aia and sensor watercolor character sheets.
No key, weapons, logos, words, police, military or authority symbolism.

## Cops / Crossplane

Create a canonical-style Oki Universe character sheet for Cops, the
personified face of Crossplane Composition. Cops is a small, friendly
copper-orange beaver-inspired architect-composer with teal workshop clothing,
goggles, drafting tools, a layered pictographic blueprint, a conductor baton
and connected modular platform blocks. Show front, three-quarter, side and
back views plus composition, coordination, revision and handoff poses. Match
the canonical Aia and sensor sheets and the Captain concept. Cops is not a
police or policy character. No key, badge, police equipment, weapons, logos or
words.

## Scene 05 / First meeting

Create a richly detailed Oki Universe harbor-shipyard scene after human
approval. Preserve canonical Aia and the Captain and Cops concept identities.
Aia hands over a bounded platform proposal. Cops unfolds a layered platform
blueprint and composes connected modules for network, identity, storage,
observability, platform and a cluster ship. Captain studies the cluster-ship
portion with compass and wheel beside a dry dock. Use watercolor and
colored-pencil storybook rendering, warm harbor light and a clear visual flow
from proposal to composition to cluster lifecycle. No key, police imagery,
logos, readable text, extra mascots or floating UI.
