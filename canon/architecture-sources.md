## Precedence

1. Accepted ADRs define normative architecture decisions.
2. A `CURRENT` claim additionally requires implementation or runtime evidence.
3. Proposed and draft ADRs may support only `TARGET`, `OPTION` or `EXPERIMENTAL` claims.
4. Slides, recordings and stories are derivatives and cannot establish architecture truth.
