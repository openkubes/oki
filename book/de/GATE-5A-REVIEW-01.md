## Layout system

- 3:2 landscape pages at 15 x 10 inches in the review PDF.
- One left text page and one right full-image page per manuscript scene.
- Warm paper background, dark navy text and OpenKubes blue/green/orange accents.
- Export-stable Liberation Sans typography in both PPTX and PDF.
- Full canonical images are used without cropping or content modification.
- Page numbers are unobtrusive and consistently placed on the outer edge.
- The manuscript text for Scenes 1-3 is imported verbatim except for removal of Markdown formatting markers.
