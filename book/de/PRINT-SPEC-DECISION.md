## Practical starting options

### Option A - 12 x 8 in landscape

Large illustrated-book experience. Requires 3600 x 2400 px illustration derivatives for 300 ppi. The separate cover spread depends on binding, paper and spine width.

### Option B - 9 x 6 in landscape

Compact book with the same 3:2 composition. Requires 2700 x 1800 px illustration derivatives for 300 ppi and therefore less enlargement than Option A.

### Option C - digital-first only

Publish the Gate-5C digital PDF now and defer trim, bleed, cover and color conversion until a specific printer is selected.

## Governance rule

Print-resolution derivatives are production assets, not new canonical illustrations. They must not alter story content, character identity, labels or architectural meaning.
