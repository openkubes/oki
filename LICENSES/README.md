# Licensing plan

Oki combines software, text, artwork, media and OpenKubes brand elements. They must not be licensed as though they were one material class.

## Licence split

| Material | Proposed direction | Status |
|---|---|---|
| build, QA, render and video scripts | Apache License 2.0 | TO DECIDE |
| Canon, manuscripts, books and guides | suitable Creative Commons license | TO DECIDE |
| illustrations and character assets | CC BY 4.0, unless otherwise noted | APPROVED |
| OpenKubes/Oki names, logos and marks | excluded from CC BY 4.0; separate trademark and brand policy | APPROVED PRINCIPLE; POLICY TODO |
| third-party material | original license and attribution | REQUIRED |

No generated or externally sourced illustration may become `CANONICAL` until its provenance record and rights review are `PASS`.

This file is a planning record, not a license grant.
