# Oki illustration licence decision

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Prepared:** 2026-08-08  
**Scope:** released Oki character sheets, scene illustrations and book artwork

This is an internal decision aid, not legal advice.

## Recommendation

Use **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (`CC BY 4.0`)** for released Oki illustrations, while explicitly excluding OpenKubes/Oki names, logos and trademarks from that copyright licence and governing them through a separate trademark policy.

Why this is the recommended default:

- it permits sharing, translation and adaptation, including commercial reuse;
- it requires attribution, a licence link and disclosure of modifications;
- it is broadly understandable for educational and community material;
- trademark rights are not themselves licensed by CC licences, but the publication notice should state the exclusion clearly to avoid implied permission.

Official references:

- [CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- [Creative Commons licence chooser](https://creativecommons.org/chooser/)
- [Creative Commons FAQ on trademarks](https://creativecommons.org/faq/)

Creative Commons notes that a CC licence cannot be revoked for copies already distributed under it. The project owner must therefore make this decision deliberately.

## Proposed publication notice

> Except where otherwise noted, the text and illustrations of the Oki Universe are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. OpenKubes, Oki, associated names, logos and source-identifying marks are excluded from this licence and remain subject to the OpenKubes trademark and brand policy. AI-assisted creation and material modifications are documented in the asset provenance records.

## Attribution proposal

> “Oki Universe” by the OpenKubes community, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Source: `https://github.com/openkubes/oki`. Changes must be indicated.
