## Production rules

1. Generate and review scenes in small batches rather than declaring all 18 canonical at once.
2. Reuse a canonical keyframe unchanged when it already fulfils the final scene brief.
3. Every new scene receives a versioned prompt, provenance record, SHA-256 integrity record and conformance review.
4. No scene changes the frozen story or introduces a new architecture claim.
5. Current, target, registration, evidence, authority, observability and recovery boundaries remain exactly as defined by the Canon.
6. Scene 17 must show its egg and lifecycle unmistakably inside the visible `MORGEN` / target context.
7. Human approval is required before any scene becomes `CANONICAL`.

## Proposed batches

- **Batch A:** scenes 1-4, establishing the world and validating KF-01/KF-02 continuity
- **Batch B:** scenes 5-9, management, contracts, sensors, creation and registration
- **Batch C:** scenes 10-13, readiness, observability, Aia and human authority
- **Batch D:** scenes 14-18, platform family, local learning, recovery, target and closing
