# KF-01 - Oki is born - candidate 01

Create one finished landscape children’s-book illustration, approximately 3:2, with generous storytelling space and no panel grid.

Use the attached canonical Oki character sheet as the exact identity reference. Preserve Oki’s teal island-spirit silhouette, leafy hair, large warm eyes, small orange nose and cheeks, ochre-yellow work jacket, teal shorts, brown boots and work satchel. This is story artwork, not a character sheet.

Scene: dawn in a wide physical-or-virtual machine landscape before the island world exists. Friendly adult human builders stand among servers, cables and softly blinking machines. One builder has placed a capability-oriented blueprint on a worktable. Turquoise and blue-green lines awaken between the machines, flow over the ground, and organize themselves into one new island with one unmistakable town hall at its center. Oki is being born as the personified form of that entire new island: compose Oki and the island as one continuous visual subject. His teal form rises naturally out of the same glowing terrain and network lines at the island’s near edge, while the shape, light and vegetation of the island echo his silhouette. It must not look as if a pre-existing Oki character merely lives on or visits the island. The humans are visibly the origin builders; Oki has just opened his eyes for the first time, curious and delighted rather than all-knowing.

Architecture truth: this is exactly one Kubernetes cluster and therefore exactly one town hall. Oki represents the whole host cluster, `ok-infra`; he is not a pod, VM or separate resident. There is no second cluster, no guest island, no cluster egg, no Aia and no sensors.

Composition: cinematic wide establishing shot, slightly elevated viewpoint. Place the newly formed Oki-island as the clear focal point, with builders and machines supporting the origin story. The single town hall should be clearly readable but smaller than the overall island. Show a gentle path from the human blueprint toward the forming island. Emotion: wonder, first breath, responsible curiosity.

Visual style: premium hand-painted European children’s-book illustration; soft watercolor and gouache on subtly textured warm paper; delicate ink-and-pencil contours; luminous dawn mist; warm, timeless, tactile and inviting; detailed enough for a full-page book spread but never glossy 3D, photorealistic or vector-flat. Keep the palette aligned with the character reference: teal and blue-green for Oki, natural earth greens for infrastructure, warm ochre for builders and early sunlight.

Do not include any words, labels, letters, UI, logos, trademarks, watermark or decorative border. Do not add extra characters. Do not create extra town halls. Do not show Oki standing independently on the island like its inhabitant.
