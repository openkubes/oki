## Prompt

Create one polished 3:2 landscape diptych that proves Mio's Current and Target
roles without changing the canonical Mio identity.

The left panel is present-day Current and carries exactly the heading **HEUTE**.
Show canonical Mio on Mio's management island beside, but clearly separate
from, Mio's blue-roofed town hall. Across a short safe bridge is an already
complete workload island with its own boundary, lit paths, mature plants and
its own town hall. A human builder stands with the already existing workload
island and formally introduces it toward Mio. Mio holds an open registration
map. A new cyan point appears in Mio's connected-point chest constellation and
a calm relationship line follows the bridge. Oki may appear only as a small
observer on outer host-island ground and never performs Registration.

The right panel is future Target and is completely enclosed by a luminous
blueprint frame carrying exactly the heading **MORGEN**. Show the same canonical
Mio with a confident responsible posture beside Mio's separate town hall and a
large declared plan or contract on a worktable. A calm cyan pulse travels from
the plan through ordinary tools and prepared infrastructure toward a closed
cluster egg on a separate prepared foundation. Human builders remain visibly
involved. The egg is not magically hatching. Every target action remains
inside the `MORGEN` frame.

Use premium hand-painted watercolor and colored-pencil children's-book style,
the exact Oki universe, fine dark-teal linework, warm sunlight and rich island
detail. Preserve canonical Mio exactly in both panels. Mio, Mio's island
boundary and Mio's town hall must remain separate concepts. The workload island
already exists and has its own light before Registration. No Authority key,
crown, wand, magic birth ray, Kubernetes logo, green approval check or Aia.

Exact text: `HEUTE` and `MORGEN` only. No other labels or watermark.
