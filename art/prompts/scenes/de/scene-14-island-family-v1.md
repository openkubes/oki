# Scene 14 - Die Familie der vielen Inseln

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: full-page German children's-book scene, 3:2 landscape
Primary request: Show four independent platform-island clusters that share a visual language of promises while remaining visibly different: shared services, AI, robotics and IoT.
Input images:
- Image 1: canonical closing archipelago reference; preserve the watercolor world, island/town-hall metaphor and shared-services vocabulary.
- Image 2: canonical Oki character identity reference; preserve Oki exactly.
- Image 3: canonical Aia character identity reference; preserve Aia exactly.
Scene/backdrop: A luminous afternoon panorama over four nearby but clearly separate islands. Oki and Aia stand on a small neutral overlook in the foreground, admiring the family rather than owning it. Thin warm routes across the water suggest shared contracts, not merged infrastructure.
Subject: Exactly four established platform islands are clearly countable. Every island has exactly one unmistakable town hall and its own shoreline. Island one is a bustling supply harbor with a secure vault-like stronghouse, registry warehouse, storage quay and lighthouse. Island two has a humane AI workshop with abstract model diagrams and helpful machines. Island three has friendly robots moving on protected marked paths. Island four has small edge devices, radio beacons and distant sensor stations. Their silhouettes, palettes and activity differ, while small matching promise-book medallions near each harbor show that all understand the same guarantees.
Style/medium: premium hand-painted European children's-book watercolor and gouache, delicate pencil-and-ink contours, warm paper texture, matching the canonical world exactly.
Composition/framing: broad readable panorama; four islands separated by water; all four town halls visible and easy to count; Oki and Aia small enough that the island family remains the subject.
Lighting/mood: welcoming, varied, cooperative and dependable.
Color palette: natural sea blues and greens; shared-services amber; AI blue-violet; robotics teal-orange; IoT warm green; Oki teal/ochre; Aia lilac/violet.
Architecture truth: Each named platform is an independent Kubernetes cluster in this metaphor and therefore has exactly one town hall. Shared services stay visible on the supply-harbor island. Similar contracts do not make the islands copies.
Constraints: no fifth island; no second town hall on any island; no town hall on a lighthouse, warehouse, robot, device, boat or foreground overlook. No single island represents all of OpenKubes. No authority key, automatic repair, product logo, readable label, caption, UI, watermark or decorative frame.
Avoid: copying the four islands; connecting them into one landmass; turning the scene into an infographic; making Oki or Aia giant owners; hiding the shared-services harbor.
