Scene/backdrop: The storm has just passed. Dark clouds recede over rough water. The outer Mother Infra / Oki shore is scarred but stable. Across a short channel, the separate blue management island is damaged: its one town hall is being rebuilt and several basic roads are incomplete.
Subject: Oki stands safely outside the damaged management island holding a dry bundle of declared building plans, offering them to human builders. The human builders—not Oki, sensors or magic—repair the town hall and basic roads. Farther to one side, in a clearly separate protected stone shelter, sits a closed memory chest with a backup/recollection symbol. A second human carries a carefully retrieved memory bundle from that safe shelter toward the rebuilt island. Some relationship lines and access bridges remain faint, disconnected or unfinished, showing that reconstruction has not automatically restored everything.
Style/medium: premium hand-painted European children's-book watercolor and gouache, delicate pencil-and-ink contours, warm textured paper, canonical world continuity.
Composition/framing: strong left-to-right logic: stable external Oki anchor with plans; human reconstruction in the center; separate safe memory source to the right. The rebuilding process is visible and incomplete, not magically finished.
Lighting/mood: honest, resilient and hopeful after danger; relief without false certainty.
Color palette: retreating storm blue-gray; Oki teal/ochre; management blue; human workwear amber; small warm recovery lights.
Architecture truth: declared configuration can support rebuilding only when recovery inputs exist. Rebuild is not full state restoration. Relationships, access and memory require separate deliberate backup and restore work.
Constraints: exactly one town hall on the host cluster and exactly one damaged/rebuilding town hall on the management cluster. The memory shelter/chest is not a cluster and has no town hall. No automatic complete recovery, no magical beam, no sensor remediation, no green all-clear, no authority key, no product logo, readable caption, UI, watermark or decorative frame.
Avoid: Oki rebuilding everything alone; plans containing all memories; the chest teleporting state automatically; a fully pristine management island; multiple management town halls.
