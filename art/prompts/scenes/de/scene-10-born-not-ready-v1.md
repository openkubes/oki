# Scene 10 - Geboren ist nicht bereit

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: full-page German children's-book scene, 3:2 landscape
Primary request: Tell a clear before-and-after readiness story: a workload island can exist while promised outcomes are still missing; the human build team repairs the missing things; only afterward do the four sensors verify again and Proof raises the green evidence check.
Input images:
- Image 1: canonical sensor-set identity reference; preserve Scout, Meter, Check and Proof exactly.
- Image 2: canonical Oki character identity reference; preserve Oki exactly.
- Image 3: canonical Scene 9 setting and workload-island reference; preserve the registered workload island and watercolor world.
Scene/backdrop: One child-readable double-vignette page showing the same workload island at two consecutive moments, separated by a gentle vertical sweep of sea mist rather than a hard technical diagram. Left is the first inspection: a path ends in sand, one important door is closed, and a small signal beacon flickers. Right is after the builders' work: the path is complete, the door is open and the beacon shines steadily.
Subject: In the left vignette the four sensors observe and record, while Proof keeps the green check lowered. Human builders with tools are visibly responsible for the repairs in the transition between the two moments. In the right vignette the builders have stepped aside; Scout, Meter and Check verify the outcomes and Proof raises the green check. Oki watches with an understanding expression.
Style/medium: premium hand-painted children's-book watercolor and gouache, delicate ink outlines, subtle paper texture, same world and character finish as canonical Scenes 7-9.
Composition/framing: clear left-to-right story sequence with generous separation between the two moments. The workload island has one town hall in each temporal vignette because it is the same island shown twice, not two clusters. Keep each action easy to read at book size.
Lighting/mood: left slightly uncertain but not frightening; middle purposeful; right warm, stable and reassuring.
Color palette: natural island greens and blues; evidence green only in the final right-side check; warm ochre builders.
Constraints: Sensors never repair anything. Builders perform all remediation. Proof's check summarizes evidence and is not a key. The island is already born in both moments; no egg or hatching. A successful build action alone is not depicted as readiness. No gate, authority key, AI, product logos, captions, labels, speech bubbles, watermark or readable text.
Avoid: a sensor holding tools; a green check in the left pre-repair moment; an automatic repair beam; making the two temporal views look like two simultaneous clusters; extra sensor characters outside the two clearly separated time vignettes.
