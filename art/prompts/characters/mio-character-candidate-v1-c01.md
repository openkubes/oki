# Mio character candidate C01 prompt

Use case: illustration-story  
Asset type: candidate character reference sheet for the Oki children's-book universe  
Reference images:

- `characters/oki/OKI-CHAR-001-v1.png` - canonical Oki; style, proportions, face language and finish
- `characters/aia/AIA-CHAR-001-v1.png` - canonical Aia; story universe and sheet layout
- `characters/sensors/OKI-SENSOR-SET-001-v1.png` - canonical sensors; silhouettes Mio must not duplicate

## Prompt

Design Mio, the personification of the OpenKubes management cluster
`ok-mgmt`. Create one polished character sheet that belongs naturally beside
the canonical Oki and Aia references while giving Mio a clearly independent
identity.

Mio is a friendly, compact, orderly young island spirit in strong navy blue
with light-blue accents. Mio is conscientious, caring, curious, slightly
over-eager, and loves maps, relationships, and order. Give Mio a smooth,
rounded, recognizable silhouette with a small swept wave-like crest made of
three soft rounded shapes; do not give Mio Oki's leafy spikes, Aia's rabbit
ears, or the sensor creatures' silhouettes. Use large warm brown eyes, subtle
warm peach cheek marks and expressive eyebrows.

Mio wears a neat navy field jacket or vest with light-blue piping, practical
trousers and small boots, visually related to Oki and Aia's explorer clothes
but more organized and compact. A tidy map folio or rolled connection map may
appear as a prop. No crown, throne, magic wand, key, staff, weapon, logo,
Kubernetes mark, or written technical label.

On the center of Mio's clothing, show a small luminous blue relationship-light
core: several cyan-blue points linked into a gentle circular constellation with
one calm pulse line. It must read as connections and promises, not as a red
anatomical heart, life-support device, power reactor, magic gem, or authority
badge.

Use a cream watercolor-paper background. The top row contains four consistent
full-body turnaround views of exactly the same Mio design: front,
three-quarter, side, and back. The bottom row contains five consistent
bust/expression studies: curious, unsure, focused, caring, and quietly
responsible. In one three-quarter view Mio may hold the tidy map folio. Keep
each view separated with generous whitespace and no scene background.

Style: hand-painted watercolor and colored-pencil children's-book character
design, subtle paper grain, clean dark-teal linework, soft warm lighting and the
exact visual family of the supplied references. Use a 3:2 landscape character
sheet. Full bodies and shoes remain visible.

No text anywhere. Show one character only. Preserve identity, costume,
proportions, core symbol, crest and colors across all nine views. Do not attach
an island or town hall to Mio's body and do not imply that Mio creates or
magically hatches clusters. Avoid an Oki recolor, rabbit, cat, hedgehog, robot,
superhero, monarch, wizard, red heart, glowing reactor, anatomical heart,
crown, key, wand, excessive machinery, armor, logos, labels, typography,
watermark, photorealism or 3D rendering.
