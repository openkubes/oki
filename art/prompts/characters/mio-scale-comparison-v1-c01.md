## Prompt

Create a clean, publication-quality scale lineup of the three supplied
OpenKubes/Oki characters, preserving their established identities exactly.

Show exactly three characters, left to right: Oki, Mio, Aia. Each appears once,
as a full-body front or very slight three-quarter standing pose, calm and
friendly, with arms relaxed enough that clothing and body proportions remain
readable.

All three stand on exactly the same ground baseline. Oki and Mio have
approximately the same head-to-boot body height, with Mio at most five percent
shorter and slightly more compact. Aia has approximately the same eye level and
body height as Mio when the long ears are excluded; Aia's ears naturally extend
above the others. No character is dominant, giant, parental, royal or tiny.
The lineup must feel like three peers in one children's-book ensemble.

Preserve Oki's exact teal face, leaf-like crest, ochre explorer jacket, tool
backpack, trousers and boots. Preserve Mio's exact blue face, three-part swept
crest, navy organized field clothing, brown backpack, boots and subtle
connected-point relationship-light constellation. Preserve Aia's exact
lavender rabbit identity, long ears, purple explorer outfit and paper-filled
backpack. Do not redesign or hybridize the characters.

Use the exact hand-painted watercolor and colored-pencil storybook family of
the supplied sheets, subtle warm cream paper grain, dark linework and soft warm
studio lighting. Compose as a 3:2 landscape with generous equal spacing, full
bodies and shoes visible. Add one very faint straight neutral baseline under
all six feet and one very faint horizontal eye-level guide behind them, with no
measurement numbers.

No text, labels, names, logo or watermark. Do not add interactions that obscure
scale. Avoid hybridized characters, copied clothing, an Oki recolored blue,
Mio with rabbit ears or leafy spikes, Aia without long ears, extra characters,
sensors, town halls, islands, eggs, keys, crowns, magic, dramatic perspective,
sitting, jumping, depth offsets, photorealism or glossy 3D.
